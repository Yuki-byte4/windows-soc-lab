# Evidence folder

Add only evidence captured from your own lab.

Suggested filenames:
- `01-wazuh-dashboard-redacted.png`
- `02-agent-active-redacted.png`
- `03-windows-security-event-redacted.png`
- `04-sysmon-event-redacted.png`
- `05-investigation-timeline-redacted.png`

Friendly safety recommendation whoever reads this before publishing:)
- Hide usernames that identify you or other people.
- Hide email addresses, public IPs, tokens, passwords, keys, and private host details.
- Do not upload full raw `.evtx` or log exports unless you have checked them for sensitive data.
- Keep the timestamp and event fields needed to support your conclusion.
- Add a short caption explaining what the screenshot proves.

A screenshot of an installed dashboard proves installation, not that a detection works.
